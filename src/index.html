📖 Zoodoku Rules

Your goal is to safely place exactly one animal per row, column, and colored region!

1
One Animal Per Row & Column: You cannot place multiple animals in the same line of the grid.
2
One Animal Per Color Region: Every continuous pastel region must host exactly one animal.
3
No Adjacency (No Diagonal Touching): Animals cannot touch each other, even diagonally in any direction!
4
Mark or Place: Single tap or drag across tiles to add bold X exclusions. Double-tap still places an animal when you are confident.
